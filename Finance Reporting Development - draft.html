How we define the main measures

  • Gross marginRevenue minus cost of sales, as a share of revenue. Shows how much each sale earns before overheads.
  • EBITDAEarnings before interest, tax, depreciation and amortisation. Compares operating performance across entities and years.
  • Budget varianceActual minus budget, in value and percent. Points budget owners to the lines that need explaining.
  • DSODays of sales outstanding in receivables. A rising figure means customers are paying more slowly.
  • DPODays of purchases outstanding in payables. Helps plan supplier payments without straining cash.
  • Forecast accuracyHow close the forecast came to the actual result. Tells you how far to rely on the next forecast.

FAQ

Power BI for finance reporting: your questions answered

What is Power BI for finance reporting?

It is the use of Microsoft Power BI to build P&L, balance sheet, cash flow, budget and other finance reports that refresh from your ledger, instead of being rebuilt in Excel every month.

Can Power BI replace our month-end Excel pack?

Yes, for most packs. Board-ready paginated reports cover the printed pack, and finance can still analyse the data in Excel.

Which accounting systems can you connect to?

Most ERPs and accounting systems, plus bank data, budget files and Excel. We confirm yours in the first review.

Will the reports match our trial balance?

Yes. Every report is reconciled to the ledger before go-live, and we run one month-end close in parallel with your current pack.

Can it handle several entities and currencies?

Yes. Group companies, intercompany eliminations and multiple currencies can sit in one model, with AED as the base.

Who can see sensitive figures?

Only the people you approve. Row-level security limits each user to the entities and cost centres they own.

Can we ask the reports questions in plain language?

Yes. Copilot in Power BI answers questions with a chart or summary. It depends on your Microsoft licence and capacity, which we check in the first review.

Why use Power BI in finance instead of other financial reporting software?

Power BI connects to almost any source, works with Excel and Microsoft 365, and includes AI and security features at a lower licence cost than most dedicated financial reporting software.

Is our financial data secure in Power BI?

Yes. Data stays in your Microsoft tenant, access is controlled by role, and every report view can be audited. Sensitive figures such as payroll or group results are visible only to approved users.

Can budget owners see only their own numbers?

Yes. Row-level security in Power BI means each budget owner opens the same report but sees only their own cost centres, while finance sees the whole organisation.

Can we start with one report and add more later?

Yes. Many clients start with the P&L or cash flow report, then add budget, ageing and consolidation pages in Power BI as the model grows.

How long does it take, and what does it cost?

Both depend on the number of reports, entities and sources, the finance report automation you need and the state of your chart of accounts. We give a timeline and an estimate after the first review.
