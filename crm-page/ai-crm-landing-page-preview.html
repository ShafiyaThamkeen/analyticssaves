CRM Solutions UAE: Frequently Asked Questions

What is AI CRM software, and how is it different from a standard CRM?

A standard CRM stores and organises customer data. AI CRM software also uses that data: it scores leads, flags deals at risk, forecasts what will close, drafts account summaries and suggests the next best action, so your team acts on insight instead of searching for it.

What AI features does an AI-powered sales CRM include?

Our AI-powered sales CRM on SugarAI includes lead and opportunity scoring trained on your own won and lost history, at-risk deal alerts, built-in forecasting, drafted account summaries and meeting prep, and next-best-action guidance for every rep.

What is a CRM, and why does a UAE business need one?

A CRM is software that keeps every customer interaction in one place, from the first enquiry to renewal and support. It helps your team follow up on time, see the full history of each customer and report on sales without spreadsheets. For UAE companies with many leads, channels and languages, it keeps everyone working from the same information.

What is the difference between CRM software, CRM services and a CRM solution?

CRM software is the platform itself. CRM services are the work around it, such as consulting, implementation, customization, integration and support. A CRM solution is the finished result: the right software, set up, connected and supported to fit your business. HAMT Infotech provides all three.

What is SugarAI, and how is it different from Sugar CRM?

SugarAI is the intelligent evolution of Sugar CRM. It keeps the sales, service and marketing platform and adds built-in predictive AI that scores leads, flags at-risk deals and drafts account summaries automatically.

Can SugarAI work if our customer data is incomplete?

Yes. SugarAI is built to give useful predictions from partial or ageing records, so you do not need a perfect database before you start. Data improves as your team uses the system.

Can you connect the CRM to our ERP and other systems?

Yes. CRM integration is a core part of our work. We connect SugarAI with ERP and finance platforms, your website, email and marketing tools, so quotes, orders and customer details move between systems automatically.

Can the CRM be customised for our industry?

Yes. We start from an industry blueprint with the fields, stages, reports and integrations your sector needs, then fit it to your business. Where your process is unusual, we build custom fields, modules and plugins inside SugarAI.

Do you support us after go-live?

Yes. A dedicated support team takes over at go-live and works to the service levels in your contract. Requests go through a single service desk and are reviewed with you regularly.
