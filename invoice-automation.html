Why automate accounts payable

More suppliers and more entities, but the same manual AP process

A capable AP team copes with a few hundred invoices a month. Then the business adds a branch, a warehouse, a second entity or another country. Invoices arrive by email, courier and WhatsApp, in Arabic and English, some still off dot-matrix printers.

Approvers travel, month-end turns into a scramble, and controls that worked at a smaller size start to slip.

Payments you cannot recall

Stalled approvals

How automated invoice processing works

The journey of an invoice, from inbox to ERP

Six steps, each one recorded. Your team steps in only when a check fails or an approval is due.

01

Capture

Invoices are collected on arrival from email, scanners, shared folders or your e-invoicing provider into one controlled queue.

  • Paper, PDF, image and structured e-invoices
  • One register of every invoice received
02

Read

HAMT AI extracts header, line, tax and supplier data in Arabic or English and files the invoice under the right supplier.

  • Handles faded, skewed and dot-matrix prints
  • Uncertain values go to review instead of being guessed
03

Verify with three-way matching

Each invoice is checked for completeness, matched to its purchase order and goods receipt, and tested against your VAT and business rules.

  • Two-way or three-way, with your tolerances
  • Supplier TRN and VAT amounts validated
04

Approve

Rules route it by entity, cost centre, value or category, with deputies and escalation for leave and travel.

  • Multi-level approval limits you define
  • Approval from a browser or phone
05

Post

Approved invoices reach your ERP with posting data prepared, in time for supplier payment terms.

  • PO-backed invoices post without manual entry
  • Early-payment discounts flagged before they lapse
06

Archive

Every invoice is stored with its full history, retrievable for the whole retention period.

  • A complete record of every change and approval
  • Retention rules set by entity and country

Invoice automation FAQs

Questions finance leaders ask us

What is invoice automation, and how does it differ from accounts payable automation?

Invoice automation uses software and AI to handle supplier invoices from arrival to ERP posting: capture, matching to purchase orders and receipts, approvals and archiving. It replaces manual keying and chasing, cutting processing time, errors and cost. Accounts payable automation is broader, adding supplier onboarding, payment runs, reconciliations and AP reporting. We start with invoice processing and can extend across purchase-to-pay.

How does AI invoice automation work, and how is it different from basic OCR?

An invoice is captured by email, scanner or e-invoice, read by HAMT AI, checked against the purchase order, goods receipt and VAT rules, approved, then posted to your ERP. OCR only reads text. Intelligent document processing adds invoice data extraction that understands layouts, line items and suppliers, scores its confidence and learns from corrections. Arabic, English and mixed-language invoices, including faded, skewed and dot-matrix prints, are read as standard.

What is HAMT AI, and do people still review invoices?

HAMT AI is HLB HAMT's own AI: Human-integrated, Automation, Machine learning and Transformation. Clean invoices flow through. Uncertain or failed ones go to a reviewer with the reason attached, never guessed, and each correction retrains the models. HLB HAMT builds and supports it, so the team implementing your rollout also tunes it.

Is it suitable for mid-sized companies and large enterprises, and how does it fit our ERP?

Yes. Mid-sized companies usually start in the cloud with ready-to-use workflows and one or two entities; large enterprises add multi-level approvals, enterprise ERP integration, private hosting and shared service centres. Both run on one platform, so you never switch systems. We integrate with ERP, accounting and in-house systems through APIs or file imports, agreed with your IT team during scoping, and approvers use our web and mobile screens with no ERP access. Hosting can be cloud, private cloud or your own servers, with data kept in the UAE if needed and the architecture documented before go-live. Unlike standalone invoice processing software, our team scopes and builds it all with you.

Is it ready for UAE e-invoicing?

Yes. UAE e-invoicing is introduced on the Peppol-based PINT AE standard: from 1 January 2027 for businesses with revenue of AED 50 million or more, 1 July 2027 for others and 1 October 2027 for government entities [CONFIRM], through Accredited Service Providers. We receive structured invoices from your provider and process them alongside scans and PDFs.

How long does implementation take, and how much does it cost?

A focused rollout with one entity, one ERP and standard formats can usually go live within a few weeks [CONFIRM]. Group programmes roll out entity by entity over several months, with the first entity live early. Cost depends on volumes, users, entities, hosting and ERP integration.
