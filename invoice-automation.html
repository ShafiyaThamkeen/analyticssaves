The learning cycle

Every reviewer correction makes HAMT AI better at your invoices

Most tools extract data and stop. HAMT AI keeps learning from the people who check its work, and that is what sets our invoice automation apart.

  1. 1

    Flag

    HAMT AI scores its confidence on each field and holds uncertain values for review instead of guessing.

  2. 2

    Correct

    A reviewer fixes the value beside the original invoice. The change and the reviewer are recorded.

  3. 3

    Retrain

    Approved corrections feed the models for your setup, tested before an update goes live.

  4. 4

    Improve

    The same supplier, layout or wording is read correctly next time, so fewer invoices need review.

A reviewer screen built for one-pass checks

Reviewers see the invoice on one side, the extracted fields on the other, and the reason each field was flagged. They confirm or correct and move on. Because every correction is kept, HAMT AI learns your suppliers' quirks: a branch name, an Arabic spelling, a tax line in an unusual place.

Exception handling that gets smarter

Exception handling is where most automation projects stall. We design the review queues, roles and thresholds with you, then track the review rate as a service measure, so the benefit is proven rather than assumed.

  • Corrections are logged with who made them and when.
  • Retraining is supervised by HLB HAMT, not left to run unchecked.
  • Data handling is agreed with your IT team before go-live.

Invoice automation FAQs

Questions finance leaders ask us

What is invoice automation?

Invoice automation uses software and AI to handle supplier invoices from receipt to ERP posting: capturing the data, matching it to purchase orders and receipts, routing approvals and archiving. It replaces manual keying and chasing, and forms part of accounts payable automation.

How is AI invoice automation different from basic OCR?

OCR only reads text. AI invoice automation adds intelligent document processing, so invoice data extraction understands layouts, line items and suppliers, scores its own confidence and learns from corrections. HAMT AI also applies your matching and VAT rules.

What is HAMT AI?

HAMT AI is HLB HAMT's own AI. HAMT stands for Human-integrated, Automation, Machine learning and Transformation. In invoice automation it reads invoices, applies your controls and learns from reviewer corrections. It is built and supported by HLB HAMT, so the models, rules and controls are tuned by the same team that implements your rollout.

Do people still review invoices?

Yes, by design. Clean invoices flow through, while uncertain or failed ones go to a reviewer with the reason attached. Each correction retrains the models, so reviewers see fewer invoices over time.

Which ERPs does it integrate with?

We integrate with SAP, Oracle, Microsoft Dynamics 365, Sage, Odoo, Zoho Books and in-house systems through APIs. Unlike standalone invoice processing software, our team scopes and builds the integration with your IT team.

How long does implementation take?

It depends on the number of entities, invoice types and your ERP. Our assessment sets the plan, usually starting with one entity or supplier group so you see results early, then scaling out. Group rollouts go entity by entity.
