The challenge

Why HR teams outgrow spreadsheets and turn to HR dashboard solutions

HR data sits in the HR system, payroll, attendance and recruitment trackers. Pulling it together takes days, and the numbers have moved by the time the report is ready. HR dashboard solutions connect those sources once and keep the picture current.

Where spreadsheets fall short

Data in too many places

Every monthly report means exporting, copying and reconciling files from several systems.

Reports that arrive late

Leadership decides on last month’s figures because this month’s are still being prepared.

Numbers that do not match

HR and finance count headcount and attrition differently, so meetings start with a debate.

Warning signs missed

A team losing people or running on overtime shows up only when someone thinks to look.

What changes with a dashboard

  • One version of the numbersHR, finance and leadership read the same agreed figures.
  • Hours back every monthTime spent assembling reports goes into understanding the figures, helped by AI summaries that explain what changed.
  • Earlier actionAI flags a rise in resignations or absence in the week it starts, while there is still time to respond.
  • Decisions you can explainEvery figure traces back to its source.

What we build

Custom HR dashboards for every part of your workforce

Each dashboard starts from a question your team is asked and uses AI to go one step further: why it happened and what is likely next. We build custom HR dashboards and agree what every number means before any data is connected.

The dashboards

Workforce overview
Headcount, joiners, leavers and average tenure by department, location and grade, with net hiring month by month. AI forecasts project headcount forward from your own history.
Attrition and retention
Leavers by team, tenure band and manager, with exit reasons and early signs of teams at risk. AI key influencer analysis shows which factors, such as tenure or overtime, are most linked to people leaving.
Recruitment
Open roles, time to hire, offer acceptance and cost per hire, from approved vacancy to joining date. AI flags a sudden drop in offer acceptance.
Payroll and compensation
Payroll cost, overtime and allowances by department, with pay compared across grades and roles. AI forecasts next quarter’s payroll cost from current trends.
Attendance and leave
Absence rates, leave balances and overtime hours by team and by month. AI anomaly detection highlights unusual absence before it becomes a staffing gap.
Performance and engagement
Ratings, promotions, training and survey results side by side, with AI-written summaries that explain the picture for each team.

How we define the main measures

  • HeadcountActive employees at a point in time, grouped any way you need.
  • Attrition rateLeavers divided by average headcount for the period.
  • Time to hireDays from an approved vacancy to an accepted offer.
  • Offer acceptance rateAccepted offers divided by offers made.
  • Absence rateDays absent divided by available working days.
  • Payroll cost per employeeTotal payroll cost divided by average headcount.

FAQ

HR dashboard development services: your questions answered

What are HR dashboard development services?

They cover the design and build of dashboards that bring HR, payroll, recruitment and attendance data into one live view.

What should an HR dashboard show?

At a minimum: headcount, joiners and leavers, attrition, time to hire, absence and payroll cost.

Which systems can you connect to?

Most HR, payroll, recruitment and attendance systems, along with databases and Excel files. HR dashboard software such as Power BI connects to a wide range of sources, and we confirm yours during the first review.

Who can see salary and personal data?

Only the people you approve. Access is set by role, so a manager sees their own team and the HR team sees the organisation.

Can we ask the dashboard questions in plain language?

Yes. Copilot and Q&A in Power BI let users type a question and receive a chart or a summary. Copilot needs the right Microsoft licence and capacity, which we check during the first review.

Will AI make decisions about our employees?

No. AI points out patterns, such as teams at higher risk of attrition. What to do about them remains a decision for your managers and HR team.

Do we need to buy new software?

You need Power BI licences for the people who will use the dashboards. Your HR and payroll systems stay as they are.

How long does it take, and what does it cost?

Both depend on the number of dashboards, the systems involved and the condition of your data. We give a timeline and an estimate after the first review.

Can the dashboards be changed later?

Yes. Measures, filters and pages can be added as your questions change. Custom HR dashboards are meant to grow with the organisation.

Can HR data sit alongside finance or customer data?

Yes. The same model can include finance figures and customer data from systems such as SugarAI CRM.
