Common questions

Insurance questions we are asked most.

How does it support audit trails and regulatory compliance?

In SugarAI, every quotation, change and approval is timestamped, with maker-checker controls and full audit trails. KYC and AML checks sit on the customer record.

Does it replace our policy administration system, or work alongside it?

Almost always alongside. Policy administration stays in the system of record. SugarAI is where sales, service and claims teams work, with data read in live and written back automatically.

Can we track broker and agent commissions separately from the policyholder relationship?

Yes. Commission and payout status sit on the broker record, separate from the policyholder, so finance reconciles payouts without exposing commercial terms.

What happens when a renewal is missed or a policy lapses?

SugarAI opens renewal pipelines automatically ahead of expiry. A lapsed policy is flagged as a reinstatement opportunity, not archived as a write-off.

Can underwriters and claims handlers work from mobile devices in the field?

Yes. A claims handler can pull up policy history before a site visit and capture the assessment on site, complete for customer, reinsurer or audit.

Can we keep our policyholder data in the UAE, or on our own servers?

Yes. SugarAI deploys as on-demand cloud, private cloud for tighter control, or on-premise where regulation requires it to be secured and scalable in every mode. This matters in insurance, where regulators expect data to stay within a jurisdiction.

Why do insurance implementations typically take longer than a standard CRM rollout?

Rating complexity and compliance sign-off add time a generic CRM project doesn’t face. A single-line SugarAI rollout lands in eight to twelve weeks; a wider programme runs five to seven months.
