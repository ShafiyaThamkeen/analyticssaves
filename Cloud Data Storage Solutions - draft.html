Why cloud storage

Why UAE companies are moving to business cloud storage

On-premise storage ties up capital, needs constant maintenance and puts every file in one building. Business cloud storage replaces that with capacity you rent as you need it, copies held in more than one data centre, and access from any device with an internet connection.

Hardware you no longer buy

No servers, disks or cooling to purchase up front. You pay for the capacity you use and adjust the plan as needs change.

One failure does not stop work

Data is kept across multiple servers and locations, so a failed disk, a power cut, a fire or a theft at the office does not take your files with it.

Teams work from one version

Staff in Dubai, Abu Dhabi or at home open and edit the same file, without emailing copies or updating each device by hand.

IT time goes back to the business

Patching, upgrades and capacity planning move to the provider and to us, which frees your IT team for work that moves the business forward.

FAQ

Cloud storage solutions in the UAE: your questions answered

What are cloud data storage solutions?

Cloud data storage solutions keep your files, databases and backups in a provider’s data centres instead of on your own servers. You reach the data over the internet, pay for the capacity you use, and rely on the provider’s infrastructure for availability and durability.

Is cloud storage secure enough for sensitive business data?

No system is risk-free, but well-configured cloud storage is typically better protected than an office server. Encryption, strict access control, multi-factor authentication, monitoring and tested backups are what make the difference, and they form part of every environment we set up.

Can our data stay inside the UAE?

In most cases, yes. Microsoft Azure, for example, runs cloud regions in Dubai and Abu Dhabi. We confirm which services and regions are available when we design your storage, and advise where a copy outside the country would strengthen your recovery position.

Should we choose Microsoft Azure or AWS?

It depends on the systems you already run, the skills in your team, where your data needs to sit and the cost profile of your workloads. We compare both in the assessment, and many clients end up using one as the main platform and the other for backup.

What is a data lake, and do we need one?

A data lake is a single store for data from all your systems, kept in a form analytics and AI tools can use. If reports take days to assemble because the data sits in separate systems, a data lake is worth considering.

How is cloud backup different from cloud storage?

Cloud storage is where your working data lives. Cloud backup keeps separate, point-in-time copies so you can restore files after deletion, corruption or a cyber incident. Most businesses need both.

Does Microsoft 365 back up our data for us?

Microsoft keeps the service running and offers limited retention, such as recycle bins. Long-term, point-in-time recovery of your mailboxes and files is your responsibility, which is why we keep a separate backup.

How do you protect backups from ransomware?

We keep at least one backup copy immutable and isolated from your production accounts, so it cannot be encrypted or deleted during an attack, and we test restores on a schedule.

Can you move our legacy ERP or HR system to the cloud?

Yes. We host and migrate ERP and HR management systems, along with their data and backups, so every branch works from one system.

We already use the cloud. Can you improve our set-up?

Yes. We review your current storage for security gaps, backup coverage, performance and cost, and then make the changes or manage the environment for you.

How long does a migration take?

It depends on data volume, the number of systems involved and how much clean-up is needed. We confirm a timeline after the assessment and migrate in stages to avoid disruption.

What does cloud storage cost?

Cost depends on how much you store, how often you access it and the level of backup and support you need. We provide an estimate with the assessment and review usage regularly to keep it under control.
