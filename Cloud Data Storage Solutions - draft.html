Why cloud storage

Why UAE companies are moving to business cloud storage

On-premise storage ties up capital and keeps every file in one building. Business cloud storage gives you capacity on demand, copies in more than one data centre and access from any device.

Hardware you no longer buy

No servers, disks or cooling to purchase. You pay for the capacity you use.

One failure does not stop work

Teams work from one version

IT time goes back to the business

Patching, upgrades and capacity planning move to the provider and to us.

FAQ

Cloud storage solutions in the UAE: your questions answered

What are cloud data storage solutions?

Cloud data storage solutions keep your files, databases and backups in a provider’s data centres instead of on your own servers. You reach the data over the internet and pay for the capacity you use.

Is cloud storage secure enough for sensitive data?

Well-configured cloud storage is typically better protected than an office server. Encryption, access control, multi-factor authentication and tested backups are part of every environment we set up.

Can our data stay inside the UAE?

In most cases, yes. Microsoft Azure, for example, runs cloud regions in Dubai and Abu Dhabi. We confirm what is available when we design your storage.

Should we choose Microsoft Azure or AWS?

It depends on the systems you run, where your data must sit and your cost profile. We compare both in the assessment.

Does Microsoft 365 back up our data for us?

Microsoft keeps the service running and offers limited retention. Long-term, point-in-time recovery is your responsibility, so we keep a separate backup.

How do you protect backups from ransomware?

We keep at least one backup copy immutable and isolated from your production accounts, so it cannot be encrypted or deleted during an attack.

Can you move our legacy ERP or HR system to the cloud?

Yes. We host and migrate ERP and HR systems with their data and backups.

How long does a migration take, and what does it cost?

Both depend on data volume, the number of systems and the backup and support you need. We confirm a timeline and estimate after the assessment.
