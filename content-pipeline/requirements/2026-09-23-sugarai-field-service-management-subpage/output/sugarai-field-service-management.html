Benefits

Six field service capabilities SugarAI brings to your operation

In SugarAI for Field Service Management, these six capabilities carry each job from the first request to the customer's sign-off.

Customer, site and equipment history in one view

The desk, dispatcher and technician see each customer's sites, installed equipment (serial, model, warranty), contracts, past visits and open cases.

Dynamic job sheets and inspection checklists

Job sheets adapt to the job type and asset, from maintenance checklists to breakdown diagnosis, and capture photos, readings and signatures.

Technician, crew and subcontractor management

Each technician and subcontractor record holds skills, certifications, zone and availability; subcontracted jobs follow the in-house standard.

Work order tracking against contract SLAs

Each work order counts down to the contract's response and resolution times, and jobs at risk escalate before a breach.

Rules-based assignment and approvals

Rules allocate jobs by skill, zone, priority and SLA: the assignment work many teams buy technician scheduling software for. Administrators edit approval rules without code.

Asset lifecycle and contract renewals

Contracts drive field service asset management: preventive maintenance is scheduled from them, warranty and AMC renewals surface early, and repeat faults are traced per asset.

Common questions

Questions field service teams ask us

How does SugarAI share parts, pricing and invoice data with our ERP?

Through integration. Your ERP owns stock, pricing and invoicing, while SugarAI, as your field service CRM software, pulls in parts and prices, then returns completed work orders for billing.

How do technicians use SugarAI on site?

Technicians work from their phone: the day's jobs, the site address for directions, the asset's history and a checklist that takes photos, readings and the customer's signature.

How are SLAs and contract entitlements tracked?

Response and resolution targets come from the customer's contract entitlements. Every work order runs its own SLA clock, with alerts before a breach and attainment reported per contract.

Can we manage subcontractors alongside our own technicians?

Yes. Each subcontractor has a record with skills and rates, closes jobs through a portal and follows your job sheet standard, so customers see one provider.

What happens when a job cannot be fixed on the first visit?

A linked follow-up work order opens, recording why: parts, access or skills. Parts requests go to stores or the ERP, and first-time fix reports show which assets need attention.

Can SugarAI plan preventive maintenance as well as reactive call-outs?

Yes. Preventive visits are generated from contract schedules and asset types, alongside reactive cases, so planners see both kinds of work in one place.

We already run SugarCRM. Does SugarCRM field service management carry over to SugarAI?

Yes. SugarCRM became SugarAI in April 2026, and existing instances keep running. We can add field service capabilities to yours, or audit and clean up an implementation that stalled.
