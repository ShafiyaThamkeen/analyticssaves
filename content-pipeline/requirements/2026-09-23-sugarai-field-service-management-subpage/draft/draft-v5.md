## Block 13: FAQs (`section#faq.wash`, first item open)

**Eyebrow:** Common questions

**H2:** Questions field service teams ask us

**Q1 (open):** How does SugarAI share parts, pricing and invoice data with our ERP?
**A1:** Through integration. Your ERP owns stock, pricing and invoicing, while SugarAI, as your field service CRM software, pulls in parts and prices, then returns completed work orders for billing.

**Q2:** How do technicians use SugarAI on site?
**A2:** Technicians work from their phone: the day's jobs, the site address for directions, the asset's history and a checklist that takes photos, readings and the customer's signature.

**Q3:** How are SLAs and contract entitlements tracked?
**A3:** Response and resolution targets come from the customer's contract entitlements. Every work order runs its own SLA clock, with alerts before a breach and attainment reported per contract.

**Q4:** Can we manage subcontractors alongside our own technicians?
**A4:** Yes. Each subcontractor has a record with skills and rates, closes jobs through a portal and follows your job sheet standard, so customers see one provider.

**Q5:** What happens when a job cannot be fixed on the first visit?
**A5:** A linked follow-up work order opens, recording why: parts, access or skills. Parts requests go to stores or the ERP, and first-time fix reports show which assets need attention.

**Q6:** Can SugarAI plan preventive maintenance as well as reactive call-outs?
**A6:** Yes. Preventive visits are generated from contract schedules and asset types, alongside reactive cases, so planners see both kinds of work in one place.

**Q7:** We already run SugarCRM. Does SugarCRM field service management carry over to SugarAI?
**A7:** Yes. SugarCRM became SugarAI in April 2026, and existing instances keep running. We can add field service capabilities to yours, or audit and clean up an implementation that stalled.
