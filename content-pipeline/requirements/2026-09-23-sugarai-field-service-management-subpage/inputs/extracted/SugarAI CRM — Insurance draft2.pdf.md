## 13. FAQ
- Eyebrow: Common questions
- H2: Insurance questions we are asked most.

Q: How does it support audit trails and regulatory compliance?
A: In SugarAI, every quotation, change and approval is timestamped, with maker-checker controls and full audit trails. KYC and AML checks sit on the customer record.

Q: Does it replace our policy administration system, or work alongside it?
A: Almost always alongside. Policy administration stays in the system of record. SugarAI is where sales, service and claims teams work, with data read in live and written back automatically.

Q: Can we track broker and agent commissions separately from the policyholder relationship?
A: Yes. Commission and payout status sit on the broker record, separate from the policyholder, so finance reconciles payouts without exposing commercial terms.

Q: What happens when a renewal is missed or a policy lapses?
A: SugarAI opens renewal pipelines automatically ahead of expiry. A lapsed policy is flagged as a reinstatement opportunity, not archived as a write-off.

Q: Can underwriters and claims handlers work from mobile devices in the field?
A: Yes. A claims handler can pull up policy history before a site visit and capture the assessment on site, complete for customer, reinsurer or audit.

Q: Can we keep our policyholder data in the UAE, or on our own servers?
A: Yes. SugarAI deploys as on-demand cloud, private cloud for tighter control, or on-premise where regulation requires it to be secured and scalable in every mode. This matters in insurance, where regulators expect data to stay within a jurisdiction.

Q: Why do insurance implementations typically take longer than a standard CRM rollout?
A: Rating complexity and compliance sign-off add time a generic CRM project doesn't face. A single-line SugarAI rollout lands in eight to twelve weeks; a wider programme runs five to seven months.
