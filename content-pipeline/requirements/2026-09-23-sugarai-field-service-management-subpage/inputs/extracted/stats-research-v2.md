- **Figure (published abstract, per search text):** access to an AI assistant raised customer-support agents' productivity by **15%**, measured as issues resolved per hour. The NBER working-paper version gives 14%. **Use the published 15%.** Do not use the novice-worker figure (34% or 30% depending on version) unless someone opens the paper and confirms which one.

- **Attribution:** **Independent academic research.** It is not about SugarAI, not about field service, and not about a CRM. It describes a generative-AI chat assistant for support agents.
- **Use:** only as a clearly labelled "research" tile if the Content Writer accepts one non-Sugar figure, for example "15%: more support issues resolved per hour with AI assistance (QJE, 2025)". It must not sit under a lede that says the figures come from SugarAI customers.

## E. Honest-framing notes for Build and Test

1. **No field-service-specific SugarAI stat exists in anything I could find.** Every SugarCRM figure above is platform-wide.
   - Word the Block 7 lede and tiles as outcomes from SugarCRM's service and case-management module (Sugar Serve), not as field or technician results.
   - Do not use "technician", "work order", "first-time fix", "job" or "maintenance reminder" in a stat label.
2. **Both bands show +30%, but they are different stats.** W2 is Sugar Sell win rate and P2 is Sugar Serve support-team revenue contribution. That does not break the no-duplicate rule, but readers may take it for a repeat.
   - Keep the labels clearly different.
   - Alternatively, swap P2 out for P4 or C1.
3. **"Actual business results from SugarCRM customers" is SugarCRM's own wording on both sheets.**
   - If a lede credits the figures to SugarCRM customers, rewrite it so it does not echo that sentence or the Insurance template's lede (see test report v4, finding 5a/5b).
   - The Block 7 lede must not claim results from field workflows.
4. **Show the source per tile if the band mixes sources.** If a Nucleus (P4) or QJE (C1) figure goes in the same band as SugarCRM sheet figures, label it inline or with a footnote. A single "SugarCRM customers report" lede would misattribute it.
5. **Verify before publishing.** Open the sugarcrm.com and sugarai.com copies of both data sheets and the Nucleus page, because neither was reachable from here.
