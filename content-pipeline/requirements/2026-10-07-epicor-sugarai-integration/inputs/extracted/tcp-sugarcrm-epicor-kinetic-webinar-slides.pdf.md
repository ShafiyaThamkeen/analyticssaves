## Slide 9. "Lead to Order Workflow: CRM & ERP Working Together" (swimlane diagram)
- Sugar lane: New Quote -> Opportunity stage "Quoting" -> Opportunity updated with Quote ->
  Opportunity stage "Won" -> Opportunity Complete.
- Epicor lane: New Draft Quote -> Quote Ready -> New Draft Sales Quote -> Sales Order Created.
- Hand-offs: Quoting (CRM) creates draft quote (ERP); Quote Ready (ERP) updates opportunity
  (CRM); Won (CRM) creates draft sales quote/order (ERP); Sales Order Created (ERP) marks
  opportunity complete (CRM).
