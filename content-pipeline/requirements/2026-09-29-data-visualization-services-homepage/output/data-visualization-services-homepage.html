COMMON QUESTIONS

Your Questions About Data Visualisation Services in UAE

What does data visualisation do for a business, and what does HLB HAMT deliver?

Data visualisation is the design of charts, dashboards and interactive reports that make a large body of figures readable in minutes and open to follow-up questions. HLB HAMT delivers the whole chain behind that: agreed KPI definitions, prepared and governed data, designed and tested views, and training and support once they are live. The aim is reporting your people open daily and act on.

How is data visualisation different from data analytics?

Analytics is the investigative work of testing and modelling data to explain a result. Visualisation is the presentation layer that turns those findings into pages a manager can act on. One without the other tends to stall. HLB HAMT offers both: we design and build the reporting, and our advanced analytics services handle forecasting, prediction and segmentation. Both run on the same governed data.

Which data analytics and visualisation software do you work with?

Our primary platform is Power BI, which we deliver through our dedicated Power BI services. As a reselling partner, we can also supply the licences you need, sized to the number and type of users. If your reporting currently runs on another tool, we migrate it and preserve the business rules behind it. Where a requirement calls for something different, we say so during the first stage.

How long does it take to build a dashboard?

It depends on scope. A single department usually takes two to four weeks, a multi-department rollout six to ten weeks, and an enterprise-scale programme three to six months. Within the first two weeks, you normally have a working prototype on your own data to review. We commit to dates at the end of the dashboard review, when we have seen your reports, sources and KPIs first-hand.

Can you bring data from several disconnected systems into one dashboard?

Yes. We regularly combine ERP, CRM, finance and HR systems with databases, spreadsheets, cloud applications and APIs. Automated pipelines pull from each source on a schedule, match records that describe the same customer, product or entity, and load them into one model. Reports then read from that model, so a figure shown in two places is calculated once.

How do you keep dashboards accurate and secure?

We build accuracy checks into every stage: each figure is validated against its source, reconciliation tests run on every refresh, and an owner is alerted if a scheduled refresh fails. Row-level security limits each user to the records their role permits. Where reports show personal data, our data protection advisory team helps us design them in line with the UAE Personal Data Protection Law, Federal Decree-Law No. 45 of 2021.4

What return can we expect from investing in data visualisation?

The gains are practical: less time spent assembling reports by hand, quicker decisions, one agreed set of figures in every meeting and earlier sight of cost and revenue movements. We do not quote a blanket percentage, because the return depends on your starting point. Instead, we agree the measures with you at the outset and report against them after launch.

Can you improve the dashboards we already have?

Yes. When existing reports go unused, the cause is usually clarity: unclear KPIs, too many visuals on one page or no obvious starting point. Slowness tends to follow from unmodelled data and heavy calculations. We review how each report is used, redesign pages around the questions people actually ask, then tune the model behind them until they load quickly.

Can you move our reports from another tool without losing business logic?

Yes. We start with an inventory of every report, measure and definition you have today, then map each calculation to its new form. Rebuilt reports run alongside the originals, and we reconcile the two until the outputs match before anyone switches over. Your users are trained on the new layouts, and the old tool can be retired once you are satisfied.
