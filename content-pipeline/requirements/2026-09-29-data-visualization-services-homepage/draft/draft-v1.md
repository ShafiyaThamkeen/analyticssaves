## S7: HLB HAMT's Data Visualisation Services (`#services`)

**Eyebrow:** WHAT WE DO

**H2:** HLB HAMT's Data Visualisation Services

**Intro:** We answer those sector needs with six offerings: our data visualisation consulting services run from the first KPI workshop to support after launch. Take one, or combine several as your reporting matures.

**01 H3:** Reporting Strategy and KPI Design
We start by auditing the reports and spreadsheets you rely on today and interviewing the people who use them. From there we agree one definition for each KPI, map which audience needs which view, and set out a phased roadmap with a platform recommendation that fits your analytics plans as well as your reporting.

**02 H3:** Data Preparation and Integration
Our engineers connect your ERP, CRM, finance, HR and operational sources, whether on-premises or in the cloud, then clean, reconcile and model them into the governed layer described above. Scheduled refresh keeps figures current, and incremental refresh loads only what has changed, which lets large histories update quickly without reprocessing years of records each night.

**03 H3:** Dashboard and Enterprise Report Development
Building starts once you have approved the wireframes. We then produce interactive dashboards and scorecards, formal paginated reports for boards and regulators, and mobile layouts for people away from their desks. Row-level security is set up as part of the build, so each user opens a report and sees only the records their role allows.

**04 H3:** Performance Tuning for Existing Dashboards
If reports are slow or ignored, we find out why before changing anything. Our engineers profile where the time goes across the pipeline, model and semantic layer, then fix it at that point: aggregation tables for large histories, fewer queries per page and leaner formulas. Where clarity is the problem, we rework layouts so the key figure is seen first.

**05 H3:** Migration and Reporting Modernisation
When you move from another reporting tool, we preserve the business rules your figures depend on and take the chance to rethink layouts that no longer serve their readers. Where your data platform is being rebuilt at the same time, reporting keeps running throughout: we advise your engineers and prove each step with a working prototype first.

**06 H3:** Training, Adoption and Support
We match training to the role. Leaders learn what each scorecard is telling them and which questions to ask; business users learn to filter and explore on their own; report builders learn to extend the model without breaking shared definitions. We hand over documentation for each model and report, then keep monitoring, fixing and enhancing your reporting as needs change.

## S14: FAQ (`#faq`, single accordion, first item open)

**Eyebrow:** COMMON QUESTIONS

**H2:** Your Questions About Data Visualisation Services in UAE

**Q1 H3:** What does data visualisation do for a business, and what does HLB HAMT deliver?
**A1:** Data visualisation turns figures into charts, scorecards and interactive reports, so patterns, exceptions and trends stand out without anyone digging through tables. HLB HAMT delivers the whole chain behind that: agreed KPI definitions, prepared and governed data, designed and tested views, and training and support once they are live. The aim is reporting your people open daily and act on.

**Q2 H3:** How is data visualisation different from data analytics?
**A2:** Analytics works out what the data means, through cleaning, statistical testing and modelling; visualisation is how that meaning reaches the people who decide. One without the other tends to stall. HLB HAMT offers both: we design and build the reporting, and our [advanced analytics services](https://hlbhamt.com/services/advanced-data-analytics-uae/) handle forecasting, prediction and segmentation. Both run on the same governed data.

**Q3 H3:** Which data analytics and visualisation software do you work with?
**A3:** Our primary platform is Power BI, which we deliver through our dedicated [Power BI services](https://hlbhamt.com/services/power-bi-partner-in-dubai-uae/). As a reselling partner, we can also supply the licences you need, sized to the number and type of users. If your reporting currently runs on another tool, we migrate it and preserve the business rules behind it. Where a requirement calls for something different, we say so during the first stage.

**Q4 H3:** How long does it take to build a dashboard?
**A4:** It depends on scope. A single department usually takes two to four weeks, a multi-department rollout six to ten weeks, and an enterprise-scale programme three to six months. Whatever the size, we put a working prototype in front of you within the first two weeks. We firm up dates after the dashboard review, when the number and condition of your sources are clear.

**Q5 H3:** Can you bring data from several disconnected systems into one dashboard?
**A5:** Yes. We regularly combine ERP, CRM, finance and HR systems with databases, spreadsheets, cloud applications and APIs. Automated pipelines pull from each source on a schedule, match records that describe the same customer, product or entity, and load them into one model. Reports then read from that model, so a figure shown in two places is calculated once.

**Q6 H3:** How do you keep dashboards accurate and secure?
**A6:** We build accuracy checks into every stage: each figure is validated against its source, reconciliation tests run on every refresh, and an owner is alerted if a scheduled refresh fails. Row-level security limits each user to the records their role permits. Where reports show personal data, our [data protection advisory](https://hlbhamt.com/services/data-privacy-and-security-uae/) team helps us design them in line with the UAE Personal Data Protection Law, Federal Decree-Law No. 45 of 2021. [4]

**Q7 H3:** What return can we expect from investing in data visualisation?
**A7:** The gains are practical: less time spent assembling reports by hand, quicker decisions, one agreed set of figures in every meeting and earlier sight of cost and revenue movements. We do not quote a blanket percentage, because the return depends on your starting point. Instead, we [agree the measures](#methodology) with you at the outset and report against them after launch.

**Q8 H3:** Can you improve the dashboards we already have?
**A8:** Yes. When existing reports go unused, the cause is usually clarity: unclear KPIs, too many visuals on one page or no obvious starting point. Slowness tends to follow from unmodelled data and heavy calculations. We review how each report is used, redesign pages around the questions people actually ask, then tune the model behind them until they load quickly.

**Q9 H3:** Can you move our reports from another tool without losing business logic?
**A9:** Yes. We start with an inventory of every report, measure and definition you have today, then map each calculation to its new form. Rebuilt reports run alongside the originals, and we reconcile the two until the outputs match before anyone switches over. Your users are trained on the new layouts, and the old tool can be retired once you are satisfied.
