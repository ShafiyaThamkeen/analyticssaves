WHY HLB HAMT

The platform matters. The partner decides the outcome.

Selecting a CRM is straightforward. Making it work alongside your ERP,
your approval structure, your finance function and your reporting
obligations is where implementations succeed or fail. For 26 years in
the UAE and GCC, HLB HAMT has worked on both sides of that equation as
advisors who understand how businesses in this region operate, and as
engineers who build the systems that run them.

What we do

We take responsibility for the whole CRM, not a part of it. Process
discovery, platform design, configuration and development, data
migration, ERP integration, user training and support after go-live
everything delivered by one team.

Who we work with

Businesses across the UAE and GCC, from growing companies with 25 users
to enterprise groups running several thousand across multiple entities.
Manufacturers, distributors, developers, banks, insurers, telecom
operators, service companies and foundations.

How we deliver

Every engagement follows a defined methodology - SugarAI's Guided
Low-Touch onboarding for a fast start, and a phased approach for complex
programmes. Scope, timelines and commercials are agreed before work
begins, with a named engagement lead throughout.

Support after go-live

A dedicated support team takes ownership at go-live and works to the
service levels set out in your contract. Requests are logged through a
single service desk, prioritised and escalated through a defined
process, with regular service reviews.

Why AI Changes CRM

SugarAI Reads the Data, Your Team Acts on It

AI is only as good as the data behind it  and this is where SugarAI is
built to work. It scores leads and opportunities the moment they’re
created, drafts and updates account summaries, and surfaces the next
best action for each rep even when the underlying records are
incomplete. Because most CRM data is partial or ageing, that matters:
teams get useful predictions from day one instead of waiting for a clean
dataset.

Predicts

Scores every lead, opportunity and account; flags deals losing momentum;
forecasts what will close.

Summarises

Generative account summaries and meeting prep, drafted automatically, so
reps stop assembling context by hand.

Guides

Next-best-action prompts instead of dashboards to dig through the system
behaves like a coach, not an archive.

Common questions

What buyers ask us before they shortlist

How is SugarAI different from other CRM platforms?

They solve different problems, and an honest comparison helps you decide
faster. Large enterprise CRM platforms are powerful, but the total cost
climbs quickly once you add sandboxes, API capacity, premium support and
specialist consultants. Simpler, easy-to-start platforms work well until
your approval flows, pricing rules or ERP dependencies become
complicated, and get harder to stretch from there. Lower-cost platforms
suit smaller teams but become harder to maintain at enterprise scale.
SugarAI sits between them: enterprise depth in customisation,
forecasting and service management, at a cost that does not escalate
with every API call, and with a deployment choice none of the others
offer. For organisations that need enterprise capability with
predictable cost and full control over where their data sits, it is
consistently the strongest fit, which is why we built our CRM practice
around it.

Can we keep our data in the UAE, or on our own servers?

Yes. You can host on the cloud with regional hosting, deploy into your
own Azure, AWS or Oracle Cloud subscription, or install on-premise in
your own data centre. Groups often mix models: trading companies on
cloud for speed, the regulated entity on-premise. It is the same product
and the same team either way. We size the infrastructure, document the
architecture for your IT and security teams, and run the deployment.

How long does implementation take, and what does it cost?

A sales-focused rollout for one company usually goes live in six to ten
weeks. A larger programme covering sales, service and marketing with ERP
integration and data migration typically runs 2.5 to 5 months, delivered
in phases so your first users are working long before the last. Cost has
two parts: SugarAI licences, priced per user per month by edition, and
our implementation fee, which depends on the number of integrations, the
state of your existing data and how much custom development is needed.
We quote a fixed price against a signed scope after a short paid
discovery, so you are not managing an open-ended project.

Will it work with our ERP?

In almost every case, yes. We have built integrations to ERPs, as well
as e-commerce platforms and telephony systems. What matters more than
the ERP name is what you want to share between the two systems. Live
stock and credit status inside the quote, approved orders written back
automatically, and invoice status visible to the sales team cover most
requirements. We agree on the exact data flows during discovery rather
than promising a generic connector.

What happens to the data in our current system?

It is migrated properly, not dumped. We profile your existing data first
(most companies find duplication and outdated records nobody has looked
at in years), and then agree what moves, what is archived and what is
cleaned on the way through. You approve the field mapping before
anything is loaded, and we run at least two full rehearsal migrations
into a test environment so your team checks real records, not samples.
Your existing system stays readable for an agreed period after go-live.

Is the AI actually useful?

It is useful when it removes a task. Lead and opportunity scoring is
trained on your own won and lost history, so it reflects how your
business really sells rather than a generic benchmark, and it does not
require a separate data subscription to be accurate. Beyond that we
apply AI to specific jobs: summarising a long case thread before a
callback, drafting a follow-up in English or Arabic, reading sentiment
across recent interactions, and extracting data from documents. We build
an AI feature when we can name the task it removes and whose time it
saves.

Who supports us after go-live?

A dedicated support team, working to the service levels agreed in your
contract. Requests are logged through a single service desk with agreed
response and resolution times, a named service manager and a defined
escalation path. When SugarAI releases new platform functionality, we
test your configurations and integrations against it so nothing changes
quietly. We also train your administrators to handle everyday
adjustments themselves, and documentation is part of every
implementation, so your CRM never becomes dependent on one individual.
