Advanced Data Analytics

Our business began in 2007 as a Data Analytics consultancy and remains a
cornerstone of our offerings within the data space. At Insight
Consulting, we believe in a collaborative approach to data analytics. We
don’t just crunch numbers – we partner with our clients to truly
understand their unique challenges, objectives, and aspirations. By
immersing ourselves in your business, we can tailor our solutions to
meet your needs and drive tangible results.

Our certified consultants have skills and experience across various
industries and functions. They are dedicated to guiding and advising you
every step of the way, ensuring you have the support and expertise
needed to succeed in all your data analytics initiatives.

Beyond consulting, we provide comprehensive application navigation,
development, and data literacy training. Our goal is to empower you and
your team to navigate analytics tools and make data-driven decisions
with ease and confidence.

[]

Whether you’re a small startup or a large enterprise, we have the
expertise and resources to help you leverage your data to its fullest
potential.

Our Services

Consulting Services: Our approach begins with a thorough business
requirements meeting to understand your objectives, challenges, and
aspirations. After selecting the appropriate technology stack, we
provide a comprehensive blueprint for the project. During the iterative
development process, we involve you at every step to ensure the solution
meets high standards of quality and reliability. We deliver the final
solution and provide training, maintenance, and ongoing support for your
investment.

[]

Technology

As part of our commitment to excellence, we’ve chosen Power BI and Qlik
as our analytics tools. These platforms offer the versatility,
functionality, and scalability necessary to address diverse use cases.

Power BI: A powerful business intelligence tool that enables users to
visualise and analyse data in real time, transforming raw data into
actionable insights. With its intuitive interface and familiarity as
part of the Microsoft family, Power BI allows organisations to uncover
trends, identify patterns, and make data-driven decisions. Whether
you’re a small business or a large enterprise, Power BI provides the
flexibility and scalability to meet your evolving analytical needs.

Qlik: Renowned for its patented associative model, Qlik allows users to
explore data relationships dynamically. By connecting data from multiple
sources, Qlik enables organisations to gain a holistic view of their
business and discover hidden insights. With its powerful data
visualisation capabilities and user-friendly interface, Qlik empowers
users to work with vast amounts of data, making informed decisions
quickly and confidently.
