COMMON QUESTIONS

Common Questions About Advanced Analytics Services in UAE

What is the difference between basic reporting and advanced analytics?

A report describes the past: sales last quarter, stock on hand, staff costs. Advanced analytics uses statistical modelling, machine learning and predictive techniques to explain the cause behind a result, estimate the likely next outcome and suggest a response. HLB HAMT builds both on one data foundation, so explanation and forecast start from the same reconciled figures.

What is ETL, and why does our business need it?

ETL stands for extract, transform, load: copy records out of systems such as your ERP, CRM, spreadsheets and APIs; fix formats, remove duplicates and apply business rules; then write the result into a warehouse or data lake. Without it, each department keeps its own version of the numbers. We turn those three steps into pipelines that start at set times, replacing manual file copying.

What is a star schema, and why does it matter for performance?

A star schema puts a fact table of transactions or events at the centre and links it to dimension tables that describe them, such as customers, products and dates. Reporting tools read this shape more efficiently than one wide, flat table, so pages load faster and totals stay consistent. We design star schemas around your KPIs using dimensional modelling.

How does predictive analytics work in practice?

A predictive model studies historical data to learn which patterns came before a given outcome, then scores new data to estimate how likely that outcome is now. We test every model against periods it has not seen before anyone relies on it, then place the predictions inside the reports your teams already open. The use cases above show typical applications.

Can you work with our existing data infrastructure?

Yes. We connect to on-premises databases, ERP and accounting systems, CRMs, cloud data warehouses, REST APIs and flat files, and we build on what you have already paid for rather than asking you to replace it. Where a source is hard to reach, we agree a workable extract with your IT team before any build starts.

What is customer segmentation, and how does it drive revenue?

Segmentation divides your customer base into groups that behave alike, whether by spending, product mix, channel or demographics. Each group can then get offers, service levels and sales attention that suit it, which supports retention and sharpens cross-selling. We run machine learning clustering across many customer attributes, then profile each cluster so your team knows who is in it and what it is worth.

Do we need a data warehouse or data lake to get started?

Not always. A focused initiative can often read directly from the sources you already run, which keeps the first project light. Larger programmes that draw on many systems are better served by a data lakehouse, which blends a lake's flexibility with a warehouse's structure; we lay it out in the layers described above. A readiness assessment tells you which route fits before you commit.

How do you protect personal data used in analytics models?

We limit each model to the personal data it needs and have our data protection advisory team review the design. That matters because churn, segmentation and lead-scoring models often draw on personal data, and in the UAE its processing is governed by the Personal Data Protection Law, Federal Decree-Law No. 45 of 2021.4 We work in line with that law from the first design session.

How does advanced analytics relate to data visualisation and Power BI?

They are three parts of one chain. Advanced analytics produces the forecasts, drivers and segments; data visualisation decides how people see them; Power BI reporting is where many teams use them day to day. HLB HAMT delivers all three, and many clients start with reporting and add prediction once their data foundation is in place.
