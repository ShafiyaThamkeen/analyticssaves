COMMON QUESTIONS

Questions UAE Teams Ask Before Choosing a Power BI Partner

What is the Power BI service, and how is it different from Power BI Desktop?

Power BI Desktop is a free, installable application for connecting to data, modelling it and designing reports.6 The Power BI service runs in the browser: access rules decide who sees each published report, data refreshes on its own schedule, and related reports are grouped into apps for each audience. Report authors mostly work in Desktop; everyone else uses the cloud. We set up both, with a workspace structure matched to how your organisation shares information.

How is Power BI licensed, and what will it cost?

Your total cost has three parts: licences, implementation and dashboard development, and data integration and modelling, each scaled to your scope. At the time of writing, Microsoft's list prices are US$14 per user per month for Pro and US$24 for Premium Per User, unchanged since 1 April 2025.8 Large deployments and external embedding run on capacity-based licensing, priced separately. As a reselling partner, we advise on the right licence mix and supply it, and PowerUP! gives you a fixed-price way to start.

Which of our systems can you connect to Power BI, and do we need a warehouse first?

Most of them. Where a standard or partner-built connector exists, we use it, and we write custom connectors for legacy systems that have none. That covers accounting packages, ERP and CRM platforms, on-premises databases, cloud warehouses and REST APIs. A warehouse is not always step one. For a handful of sources, direct connection is fine. Once data spreads across five or more systems, we recommend a lakehouse so every report works from the same cleaned tables.

Can you replace our manual spreadsheet reports with Power BI?

Yes. It is often the first thing clients ask us to fix. Rather than a team member copying figures between workbooks every week, data refreshes on a schedule and dashboards stay current. Through Power BI automation, we set alerts that email a KPI's owner the moment it moves outside its agreed range. Clients in the Emirates have taken hours of weekly manual reporting off their teams. Spreadsheets stay available for anyone who prefers to analyse there.

Do you build Arabic and right-to-left dashboards?

Yes. We design right-to-left from the first wireframe rather than mirroring a finished English report. Labels, filters, slicers and KPI descriptions appear in Arabic, while page layout, visual order and navigation all run right to left. We apply the same method to other right-to-left languages, such as Urdu and Persian. When you need Arabic and English editions of one report, both run on a single model, so their totals always agree.

Is our data secure, and can it stay in the UAE?

We configure row-level security so people see only the records they are entitled to, and data is encrypted. Power BI falls within the scope of Microsoft's ISO/IEC 27001 and SOC 2 audits.9 Data can be kept in-country in the Dubai or Abu Dhabi cloud region1, and our data protection advisory team sets each environment up in line with PDPL, DIFC and ADGM requirements. Outside the US and EU data boundaries, Power BI's AI assistant stays off unless an admin allows cross-region processing; we make that call with you.10

Do we need a data platform beyond Power BI, and when?

Not always. Power BI handles most departmental reporting on its own. Microsoft's wider data platform, which adds data engineering, warehousing and real-time analytics on a common pool of capacity, becomes worth planning for once you feed reports from large or fast-changing sources, once engineering and analyst teams want to work off a shared copy of the data, or once per-user licence spend outgrows capacity pricing. Our readiness assessment tells you where you stand, and if the move makes sense, we plan it in stages.

Does Power BI include AI features, and what do we need to use them?

Yes. Power BI includes a generative AI assistant that answers plain-language questions and drafts written summaries of report pages. It runs only on paid capacity, a higher-tier licence bought for the whole organisation; Pro and Premium Per User on their own are not enough. A tenant administrator must also switch it on, and the semantic model needs clear table names, descriptions and tested measures, which we prepare. Prompts are officially supported in English only for now, so we put Arabic into report design instead.10

Why are some Power BI dashboards slow, and can you fix ours?

Usually, yes. The tool is rarely the problem; how the report was built almost always is. Typical causes are a weak data model, inefficient DAX or queries, large datasets nobody has optimised, and missing aggregations or indexing. We work through the pipeline, the model and the semantic layer: trimming what each visual asks for, adding aggregation tables and rewriting heavy measures. Every fix is retested at realistic data volumes before the report goes back to your users.

How long does a Power BI implementation take?

Scope drives the timeline. Expect two to four weeks for a single department, six to ten weeks for a multi-department programme, and three to six months for an enterprise build on a data lakehouse. In every case you get a working prototype inside the first two weeks, so your feedback steers the rest of the build. HLB HAMT confirms the timeline after discovery, once we know the condition of your data and how many sources are involved.
