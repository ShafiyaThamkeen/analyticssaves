WHY POWER BI

Why Power BI: From Raw Data to Boardroom Decisions

Power BI is Microsoft's business intelligence platform, and it sits naturally beside the tools your teams open every day: Excel, Teams, SharePoint, Outlook, Azure and Dynamics 365. For organisations already running on Microsoft, that native fit means people adopt it with very little friction. Four capabilities do most of the work.

Capability

Self-service analytics, built in Power BI Desktop

Business users build and explore their own visuals without joining an IT queue. Underneath, DAX measures calculate the KPIs finance reports on, such as year-over-year growth, running totals and year-to-date revenue. Every page then works from one agreed definition.

Key capabilities

  • Interactive visuals
  • DAX measures for custom KPIs
  • Power Query data shaping
  • Reusable report themes
  • Arabic and English layouts

Capability

The Power BI service

It is the cloud side of the platform, where finished reports are published, bundled into apps, refreshed on schedule and secured.5 Dashboards serve leadership and reports serve analysts. Both draw on the same data, which puts every part of the business in view from one place.

Key capabilities

  • Usage monitoring

Connected to your ecosystem

COMMON QUESTIONS

Questions UAE Teams Ask Before Choosing a Power BI Partner

What is the Power BI service, and how is it different from Power BI Desktop?

Power BI Desktop is Microsoft's free Windows application for connecting to data, building the semantic model and designing reports.5 The Power BI service is the browser-based cloud platform where those reports go live: they are shared as apps, refreshed automatically and protected by access rules. Authors tend to work in Desktop, while everyone else reads and interacts with reports online. We set up both, including a workspace structure that keeps the right numbers in front of the right people.

How is Power BI licensed, and what will it cost?

At the time of writing, Microsoft lists Power BI Pro at US$14 and Premium Per User at US$24 per user per month, the list prices since 1 April 2025.10 Per-user licences suit teams where most people both view and share reports. Larger deployments, embedding for external audiences and Copilot run on Fabric capacity, which is priced separately. Our licence advisory works out which mix fits your user base, so you aren't paying for seats or capacity nobody uses.

Which data sources can Power BI connect to, and do we need a data warehouse first?

Most business systems are within reach. Microsoft's own connectors, partner-built connectors and custom builds cover sources such as SAP, Oracle, NetSuite, Dynamics 365, Salesforce, SugarCRM, Tally, QuickBooks, SQL Server, Snowflake, Google BigQuery and REST APIs. A warehouse is not always the first step. For a smaller scope with a handful of sources, connecting directly works well. Once data is spread across five or more systems, a lakehouse is usually worth it: every report reconciles to the same cleaned tables.

Can Power BI replace our manual Excel reports?

Yes, and it is often the first job we take on. Instead of someone rebuilding spreadsheets every Monday, data refreshes on a schedule and dashboards always show current figures. Power Automate can email whoever owns a figure the moment it moves outside its agreed range. For clients in the Emirates, that Monday routine used to cost hours; now it largely runs itself. Excel stays available for anyone who still prefers to analyse there.

Do you build Arabic and right-to-left dashboards?

Yes. We plan right-to-left layouts before the first visual is placed, instead of mirroring an English report at the end. Labels, filters, slicers and KPI descriptions appear in Arabic, and page layouts, visual order and navigation read right to left. The same approach works for other right-to-left languages such as Urdu and Persian. Where you need Arabic and English versions of the same report, both sit on one model and the numbers always match.

Is our data secure, and can it stay in the UAE?

Yes. Row-level security limits each user to the rows they are entitled to see, data is encrypted, and the platform sits within the scope of Microsoft's ISO/IEC 27001 certification and SOC 2 reports.11 Your data can be hosted in Microsoft's Dubai or Abu Dhabi cloud regions1, and our data protection advisory team helps set each environment up in line with PDPL, DIFC and ADGM requirements. Outside the US and EU data boundaries, Copilot is off by default unless an admin allows cross-region processing. We make that call with you.8

What is Microsoft Fabric, and should we plan for it?

Fabric is the larger Microsoft service that Power BI now belongs to. Its other workloads (real-time analytics, data engineering and warehousing) share one pool of capacity. Fabric pays off when reporting depends on several large or fast-moving sources, when engineers and analysts should query a single copy of the data, or when your per-user licence costs are climbing. For a single team reporting from a couple of systems, it can wait. Our readiness assessment tells you which situation you are in and plans any migration in stages.

What do we need to use Copilot in Power BI?

Three things. First, paid capacity: Fabric F2 or higher, or Premium P1 or higher, because Pro or Premium Per User licences alone do not include it. Second, an administrator has to enable Copilot in the tenant settings. Third, a well-prepared semantic model, with clear table names, descriptions and tested measures; that groundwork makes Copilot's answers reliable. Microsoft officially supports English prompts only at present, so we design Arabic into the reports themselves rather than into the questions.8

How long does a Power BI implementation take?

It depends on scope. A single-department rollout usually takes two to four weeks. A multi-department programme runs six to ten weeks, and an enterprise build with a data lakehouse takes three to six months. Whatever the size, a working prototype arrives within two weeks, early enough for your feedback to shape the build. Timelines are confirmed once discovery has shown the state of your data and the number of sources involved.
