## S7: HLB HAMT's Power BI Services (`#services`)

**Eyebrow:** WHAT WE DO

**H2:** HLB HAMT's Power BI Services

**Intro:** We handle every stage of a Power BI programme ourselves, from strategy through to post-launch support, and most clients use several of the services below. Your engagement model, covered further down, decides the mix.

**01 H3:** Strategy, discovery and licence planning
We start from your business goals, data needs and reporting expectations, then translate objectives into KPIs with agreed calculation rules and spot where each role lacks information. Our consultants assess BI readiness and design a scalable architecture. As a Power BI reselling partner, we also recommend and supply the right licence mix: Pro, Premium Per User or capacity.

**02 H3:** Data integration and preparation
Our engineers build ETL and ELT pipelines in Azure Data Factory, shape data marts and warehouse layers, and configure connectors and the on-premises gateway, writing custom connectors for legacy systems. We configure scheduled and incremental refresh, add near-real-time feeds where a process needs them, and move to a lakehouse once volume and system count call for it.

**03 H3:** KPI-centric modelling and dashboard development
We work model-first, not visual-first: semantic models with consistent KPI definitions and tested DAX come ahead of the first chart. On that base we build executive dashboards and analyst reports with drill-through, cross-filtering, slicers and bookmarks, plus mobile layouts and Arabic right-to-left design. See [Power BI dashboard development](https://hlbhamt.com/services/power-bi-dashboard-development/) for the full scope of this work.

**04 H3:** Performance tuning, security and governance
When reports drag, we tune across the pipeline, data model and semantic layers, cutting query load, adding aggregation tables over large fact tables and replacing heavy measures with efficient DAX patterns. On the governance side, we implement row-level security, workspace governance and access controls, so each person sees only the data their role allows and publishing stays controlled.

**05 H3:** Migration, embedded analytics and Fabric readiness
We migrate Tableau and Qlik environments to Power BI, carrying your business logic across and redesigning each visual for the new platform instead of copying screens one for one. We also move reporting off spreadsheets, embed dashboards in customer and investor portals with Power BI Embedded, and assess Fabric readiness so any move happens in planned stages.

**06 H3:** Training and continuous support
We train in three tracks: Executive, for reading and questioning dashboards; Business User, for building reports; and Developer, for data modelling and advanced DAX. Sessions run on-site in Dubai, remotely or in a hybrid format, and can be delivered in Arabic. Contextual documentation written around your own reports, plus ongoing support, keeps your team self-sufficient after handover.

## S14: FAQ (`#faq`, single accordion, first item open)

**Eyebrow:** COMMON QUESTIONS

**H2:** Questions UAE Teams Ask Before Choosing a Power BI Partner

**Q1 H3:** What is the Power BI service, and how is it different from Power BI Desktop?
**A1:** Power BI Desktop is a free Windows application for connecting to data, modelling it and designing reports. [7] The Power BI service runs in the browser: access rules decide who sees each published report, data refreshes on its own schedule, and related reports are grouped into apps for each audience. Report authors mostly work in Desktop; everyone else uses the cloud. We set up both, with a workspace structure matched to how your organisation shares information.

**Q2 H3:** How is Power BI licensed, and what will it cost?
**A2:** Your total cost has three parts: licences, implementation and dashboard development, and data integration and modelling, each scaled to your scope. At the time of writing, Microsoft's list prices are US$14 per user per month for Pro and US$24 for Premium Per User, unchanged since 1 April 2025. [8] Large deployments and external embedding run on Fabric capacity, priced separately. As a reselling partner, we advise on the right licence mix and supply it, and [PowerUP!](#packages) gives you a fixed-price way to start.

**Q3 H3:** Which of our systems can you connect to Power BI, and do we need a warehouse first?
**A3:** Most of them. We reach sources such as SAP, Oracle, NetSuite, Dynamics 365, Salesforce, SugarCRM, Tally, QuickBooks, SQL Server, Snowflake, Google BigQuery and REST APIs through standard and partner-built connectors, and write custom connectors for legacy systems nothing else covers. A warehouse is not always step one. For a handful of sources, direct connection is fine. Once data spreads across five or more systems, we recommend a lakehouse so every report works from the same cleaned tables.

**Q4 H3:** Can you replace our manual Excel reports with Power BI?
**A4:** Yes. It is often the first thing clients ask us to fix. Rather than a team member copying figures between workbooks every week, data refreshes on a schedule and dashboards stay current. Through [Power BI automation with Power Automate](https://hlbhamt.com/insights/unlocking-power-bi-automation-with-power-automate/), we set alerts that email a KPI's owner the moment it moves outside its agreed range. Clients in the Emirates have taken hours of weekly manual reporting off their teams. Excel stays available for anyone who prefers to analyse there.

**Q5 H3:** Do you build Arabic and right-to-left dashboards?
**A5:** Yes. We design right-to-left from the first wireframe rather than mirroring a finished English report. Labels, filters, slicers and KPI descriptions appear in Arabic, while page layout, visual order and navigation all run right to left. We apply the same method to other right-to-left languages, such as Urdu and Persian. When you need Arabic and English editions of one report, both run on a single model, so their totals always agree.

**Q6 H3:** Is our data secure, and can it stay in the UAE?
**A6:** We configure row-level security so people see only the records they are entitled to, and data is encrypted. The Power BI platform falls within the scope of Microsoft's ISO/IEC 27001 and SOC 2 audits. [11] Data can be kept in-country in the Dubai or Abu Dhabi cloud region [9], and our [data protection advisory](https://hlbhamt.com/services/data-privacy-and-security-uae/) team sets each environment up in line with PDPL, DIFC and ADGM requirements. Outside the US and EU data boundaries, Copilot is off by default unless an admin allows cross-region processing; we make that call with you. [10]

**Q7 H3:** What is Microsoft Fabric, and should we plan for it?
**A7:** Fabric is the wider platform Power BI now sits in, adding real-time analytics, data engineering and warehousing on a common pool of capacity. It becomes worth planning for once you are feeding reports from large or fast-changing sources, once your engineering and analyst teams want to work off a shared copy of the data, or once per-user licence spend outgrows capacity pricing. Smaller setups can wait. Our readiness assessment tells you where you stand, and if the move makes sense, we plan it in stages.

**Q8 H3:** What do we need to use Copilot in Power BI?
**A8:** You need three things. Copilot runs only on paid capacity, meaning Fabric F2 or above or Premium P1 or above; Pro and Premium Per User licences on their own are not enough. A tenant administrator then has to switch it on. Finally, the semantic model needs clear table names, descriptions and tested measures, and we prepare those because they improve Copilot's answers. English is the only officially supported prompt language for now, so we put Arabic into report design rather than into questions. [10]

**Q9 H3:** Why are some Power BI dashboards slow, and can you fix ours?
**A9:** Usually, yes. The tool is rarely the problem; how the report was built almost always is. Typical causes are a weak data model, inefficient DAX or queries, large datasets nobody has optimised, and missing aggregations or indexing. We work through the pipeline, the model and the semantic layer: trimming what each visual asks for, adding aggregation tables and rewriting heavy measures. Every fix is retested at realistic data volumes before the report goes back to your users.

**Q10 H3:** How long does a Power BI implementation take?
**A10:** Scope drives the timeline. Expect two to four weeks for a single department, six to ten weeks for a multi-department programme, and three to six months for an enterprise build on a data lakehouse. In every case you get a working prototype inside the first two weeks, so your feedback steers the rest of the build. HLB HAMT confirms the timeline after discovery, once we know the condition of your data and how many sources are involved.
