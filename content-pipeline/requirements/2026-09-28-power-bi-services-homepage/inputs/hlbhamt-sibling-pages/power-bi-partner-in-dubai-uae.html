Frequently Asked Questions

Power BI Services — FAQ

What is Power BI, and why is it the preferred business intelligence tool for UAE companies?

Power BI is Microsoft’s business intelligence platform that turns raw data into visual dashboards and reports. Ranked #1 by Gartner for 14+ consecutive years, it connects natively to Excel, Azure, Teams, SharePoint, Outlook and Business Applications, so if your company already uses Microsoft, Power BI is the natural fit.

UAE advantage: Arabic language support, RTL dashboard layouts, and data stored in Azure’s UAE data centers (Dubai & Abu Dhabi).

What's the difference between a dashboard and a report?
A dashboard is a single page of key metrics your CEO, CFO, Operation Heads/Team and other stakeholders checks every morning. A report is a multi-page deep-dive with filters and drill-downs your analyst uses to answer why. We build both.
What is DAX and why does it matter?

DAX is Power BI’s formula language for custom KPIs — year-over-year growth, running totals, YTD revenue. It’s the difference between a basic chart and genuinely useful analytics. Our consultants write optimised DAX so your numbers are fast and accurate.

How does Power BI compare to Tableau and Qlik?

Lower cost (~$15/user/month vs Tableau ~$75), native Microsoft integration, better self-service for non-technical users, built-in AI (Q&A, anomaly detection, Copilot), and stronger Arabic/RTL support. For Microsoft-heavy organisations, Power BI delivers the fastest ROI. We also help migrate from Tableau or Qlik.

Can Power BI replace our manual Excel reports?

Yes. Power BI automates data refresh — no more copy-pasting between spreadsheets. Dashboards are always current. Pair it with Power Automate for email alerts when KPIs breach thresholds. We’ve helped UAE businesses eliminate hours of weekly manual reporting.

Does Power BI work on mobile?

Yes — iOS, Android, and Windows. View dashboards, drill into reports, set alerts, annotate, and share on the go. We design mobile-optimised layouts as standard.

Does Power BI support Arabic and RTL dashboards?

Fully. Arabic labels, filters, slicers, KPI descriptions — all right-to-left. We also support Urdu, Hebrew, and Persian. Unlike competitors who retrofit RTL, we design for it from day one.

Is my data safe? What about UAE data residency?

Power BI includes row-level security, encryption, and ISO 27001/SOC 2 compliance. Azure has data centres in UAE North (Dubai) and UAE Central (Abu Dhabi) — your data stays in-country. We configure environments to meet UAE PDPL, DIFC, and ADGM regulations.

Can Power BI help with UAE Corporate Tax and VAT?

Yes. We build dashboards tracking taxable income, deferred tax (IAS 12), VAT returns, transfer pricing, and free zone vs mainland segregation.

Our edge: We’re a licensed tax firm AND a tech consultancy — your tax dashboards are audit-ready, not just pretty charts.

Can Power BI handle Real Estate analytics in the UAE?

Absolutely. We track EOIs, deal pipelines, lead conversion, unit availability, payment plans, broker performance, and DLD compliance. Built on Azure Data Lakehouse ingesting Salesforce (60+ modules), NetSuite, SAP, Dynamics 365, Oracle, all feeding optimised Power BI dashboards.

Can it handle multi-entity, multi-currency group reporting?
Yes — consolidated P&L, balance sheets, and cash flows across entities in AED, USD, SAR, EUR, and more. Intercompany eliminations, exchange rate conversions, and IFRS segment reporting included. Our CA background ensures the financial logic is audit-ready.

What data sources can Power BI connect to?

200+ connectors — SQL Server, Azure, SAP, Oracle, NetSuite, Sugar CRM, Salesforce, Dynamics 365, Excel, SharePoint, REST APIs, Tally, QuickBooks, Snowflake, Google BigQuery, and more. We build Azure Data Factory pipelines for enterprise-scale consolidation

What is Azure Data Lakehouse and how does it help?

It combines a data lake + data warehouse in one architecture. Data flows through Bronze → Silver → Gold layers, getting progressively refined. Uses Azure Data Lake Storage, Azure Data Factory, Synapse, and Power BI. Ideal when you have data scattered across 5+ systems and need a single source of truth.

Can Power BI be embedded in our own apps or portals?

Yes. Power BI Embedded lets you put interactive dashboards inside your own web apps, investor portals, or client platforms — fully branded. Capacity-based licensing makes it cost-effective for external distribution.

What AI features does Power BI offer?

Q&A (ask in English or Arabic), Anomaly Detection, Smart Narratives, Key Influencers, Decomposition Trees, Azure ML integration, and Copilot for generative AI report creation. We help you move from what happened to what should we do next.

What is Microsoft Fabric? Should I care?

Fabric is Microsoft’s next-gen unified platform — Power BI + data engineering + data warehousing + real-time analytics, all under one roof with simplified billing. If you’re growing beyond basic dashboards, Fabric is the future. We can assess your readiness and plan the migration.

How is HLB HAMT different from other Power BI consultants in UAE and Middle East?

Finance + Technology under one roof. We’re a licensed audit, tax, and advisory firm that also builds enterprise technology. Most competitors are IT-only — they can make charts but don’t understand the numbers behind them.

Key differentiators: Global HLB International network · Deep UAE regulatory expertise (Corporate Tax, VAT, IFRS, PDPL) · End-to-end in-house delivery · Native Arabic/RTL design · Audit-ready financial logic.

Do you provide Power BI training?

Yes — Executive (reading dashboards), Business User (building reports), and Developer (data modelling, advanced DAX). On-site in Dubai, remote, or hybrid. Arabic-language training available.

How long does implementation take?

Single department: 2–4 weeks. Multi-department: 6–10 weeks. Enterprise + Data Lakehouse: 3–6 months. Working prototype within 2 weeks.

Do you support after go-live?

Yes — performance monitoring, troubleshooting, new dashboards, quarterly health checks, licence advisory, and Fabric migration support. UAE-based team, same-timezone responses (Monday–Friday).
