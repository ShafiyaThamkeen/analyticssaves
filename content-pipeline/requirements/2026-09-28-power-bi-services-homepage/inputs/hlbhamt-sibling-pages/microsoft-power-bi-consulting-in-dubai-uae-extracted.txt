Our Solutions & Services

Power BI is Microsoft’s robust suite of business analytics tools that
enables companies to visualize their data, share insights throughout
their organization, and integrate them into apps or websites. Thanks to
its user-friendly design, seamless integration options, and powerful
report automation features, Power BI is an essential tool for any
company eager to make the most of its data.

Our Power BI Consulting

At HLB HAMT, we take pride in offering a full suite of Power BI
Consulting Services that cover every stage of Power BI
implementation—from crafting a solid strategy to deployment and ongoing
support.

Power BI Strategy and Planning

Our dedicated team of Power BI specialists collaborates with you to
grasp your business goals, data needs, and reporting expectations. We
then tailor a strategy that aligns perfectly with your objectives,
making sure Power BI becomes an essential tool for informed
decision-making throughout your organization.

Power BI Implementation

We’re here to help you roll out Power BI by setting up your data
sources, building data models, and designing user-friendly dashboards
and reports. Whether you need real-time reporting or advanced analytics,
we customize your Power BI implementation to fit your unique business
requirements.

Data Integration and Automation

Power BI effortlessly connects with various data sources, including ERP
systems, CRM platforms, and databases. At HLB HAMT, we streamline your
data flows, integrate with both internal and external systems, and
create a cohesive view of your business performance, ensuring your data
is always current and easily accessible.

Custom Dashboards and Reporting

Unlock actionable insights with personalized dashboards and reports that
spotlight the key performance indicators (KPIs) relevant to your
business. Our consultants design interactive and dynamic reports that
empower users to dive deep into the details and discover significant
trends and patterns.

Power BI Training and Support

We provide thorough Power BI training to ensure your team can navigate
the platform effectively and leverage their data to the fullest. Plus,
we offer ongoing support to help you maximize Power BI’s potential,
resolve any issues, and enhance your reporting processes.

Proven Track Record

As part of the global HLB International network, we have a solid track
