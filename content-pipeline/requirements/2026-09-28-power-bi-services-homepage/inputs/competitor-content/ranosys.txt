Frequently Asked Questions

What business problems can Power BI dashboards solve?

[] []

Power BI transforms raw data into visual insights. Sales teams track
performance. Finance teams automate reporting. Retailers forecast
demand. Manufacturers monitor efficiency. Power BI answers critical
questions in real time across all industries.

How quickly can Power BI dashboards be designed and deployed?

[] []

Most Power BI dashboards launch in 2–6 weeks, depending on data
complexity and integrations. Simpler dashboards go live in days. Our
agile approach delivers working versions early for your feedback and
iterative refinement.​

Do you provide Power BI managed services and ongoing support?

[] []

Yes. Our managed services keep dashboards running smoothly and
delivering value. We monitor performance, optimize reports, handle data
refresh issues, and implement new features. We also provide 24/7 support
and user training.​

Can Power BI integrate with Dynamics 365, Azure SQL, SAP, or Salesforce?

[] []

Absolutely. Power BI connects seamlessly to Dynamics 365 for live
dashboards. Azure SQL, SQL Server, and databases integrate directly. SAP
and Salesforce connect through custom connectors. Pre-built connectors
ensure Power BI works with your entire tech stack.​

Can Power BI support real-time or near-real-time analytics?

[] []

Yes. Power BI supports real-time dashboards through streaming datasets
and automatic refresh. You can monitor live metrics and set alerts that
notify you when critical thresholds are triggered. Near real-time
capability enables swift decision-making.
