What is Power BI?

Part of the Microsoft Power Platform suite, Power BI is a cloud-based
application that helps businesses connect, visualise and use their data
for informed decision making. Power BI is Microsoft’s business
intelligence platform and data visualisation tool that allows businesses
to connect their data, prepare and model it to provide the insights and
analysis they need.

How can my business use Power BI?

Create a data-driven culture in your business with business intelligence for all

Power BI can be leveraged in a variety of ways to help you gain valuable
insights into your business operations, identify areas for improvement,
and make data-driven decisions. BCN specialise in Power BI services to
businesses of all sizes and provide training and support to ensure our
customers get the most out of the tool.

Speak to an expert today [down]

-   Data Insight Through Visualisation

    Transform your data with Power BI’s range of visually appealing
    charts, cards, data maps and more. Give your teams the insights they
    need to make better decisions, faster.

    []

-   Connect Multiple Data Sources

    Power BI connects to 150+ data sources, including Excel, SQL,
    Oracle, Google Analytics, CRM systems, EPOS systems and accountancy
    software, enabling you to generate powerful and accurate reports and
    insights. Power BI can further enable you to embed visualisations
    into apps or your website.

    []

-   Collaborate on Reports

    Collaborate and share your Power BI reports and dashboards with
    colleagues in Power BI Workspaces or in Microsoft Teams, giving your
    teams visibility and management of dashboards, reports, and data
    sheets.

    []

-   Integrations

    Power BI can be seamlessly integrate with Microsoft products like
    Excel and the other Power Platform applications. Power BI can also
    be layered across a number of stand-alone data sources or integrated
    as part of your Microsoft Azure environment.

    []

-   Surface

    Power BI can be surfaced to your business users through a host of
    end user platforms.  Power BI apps can give your team reporting on
    the go, Teams embedded reports gives your team the insights where
    they work. Power BI can be embedded into all kinds of user platforms
    such as Power Apps, SharePoint, .NET applications, portals and
    beyond with Power BI Embedded Licensing.

    []

-   Governance & Adoption

    Deploying Power BI within your organisation requires not only a data
    and analytics strategy but a strong vision for the governance and
    control of the data that is exposed to your business users.  There
    are a number of features in the Power BI platform that enable
    visibility and control of access, lineage and data source control. 
    For more advanced reporting we have a number of tools to extend and
    enhance this functionality, enabling disaster recovery, usage
    analytics, change tracking and assurance within your Power BI
    estate.

FaQS

Power BI Frequently Asked Questions

Our expert team answer some of the most frequently asked questions on
business intelligence and Power BI. Got another question? We’re here to
help.

-   What is business intelligence?

    Business intelligence allows firms to collect, examine, and turn
    unstructured data into valuable insights for strategic decision
    making. Data from diverse sources must be gathered, integrated, and
    interpreted in order to provide a complete picture of business
    operations, performance, and trends. Data warehousing, mining,
    reporting and dashboards are just a few of the tools and
    methodologies included in business intelligence (BI). 

    []

-   Who uses business intelligence?

    Numerous stakeholders within an organisation can use business
    intelligence to guide decision-maing and enhance business
    performance. BI can be used by business analysts; executive and
    senior management; operations managers, sales and marketing
    professionals, finance and accounting teams.

     

    []

-   What sources can I connect Power BI to?

    Excel, Text/CSV, XML, JSON, Folder, PDF, SharePoint folder, SLQ
    Server database, Oracle database, Power BI datasets, Power BI
    dataflows, Microsoft Dataverse.

     

    []

-   What are the advantages of using Power BI?

    Power BI dashboards offer several advantages, including:

    -   The dashboards are simple and easy to use for non-technical
        users.
    -   Real-time data updates means you always have the latest data,
        stats and KPIs
    -   Dashboards are interactive and can be customised for you
    -   Ability to share insights with others

    Plus, as its part of the Microsoft eco-system there’s seamless
    integration with other Microsoft products.
