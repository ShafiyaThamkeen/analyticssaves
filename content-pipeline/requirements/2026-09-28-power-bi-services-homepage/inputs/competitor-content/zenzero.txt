Why partner with us

Ensure seamless performance with expert Power BI support

Microsoft Power BI is a powerful business intelligence solution that
enables organisations to analyse data, create reports, and generate
actionable insights. However, ensuring optimal performance and resolving
technical challenges requires dedicated support. Our Power BI support
services provide businesses with the expertise and knowledge needed to
maintain a high-functioning Power BI environment, resolve support
requests efficiently, and enhance business processes.

With our expert BI support, we help businesses optimise their Power BI
solutions, troubleshoot issues, and provide assistance with data
management, security, and performance optimisation. Whether you need
help with Power BI Desktop, Power BI Service, or advanced integrations,
our team is here to assist.

tailored solutions

How we assist Power BI users

Our Power BI support services ensure that your organisation can fully
leverage Microsoft Power BI for enhanced data analysis, reporting, and
decision-making. Whether your team requires assistance with configuring
security settings, troubleshooting Power Query errors, or optimising
Power BI dashboards, our support experts are on hand to provide tailored
solutions that keep your business intelligence strategy running
smoothly.

We offer a comprehensive support framework, covering everything from
resolving performance issues and managing Power BI Service connectivity
to refining custom reports and assisting with data governance best
practices. Our support services are designed to eliminate
inefficiencies, reduce downtime, and ensure your Power BI solution is
always performing at its best.

For businesses requiring ongoing BI support, we provide flexible support
plans tailored to different organisational needs. These include
dedicated account managers who oversee your Power BI environment,
ensuring you always have expert guidance and a structured approach to
issue resolution. Whether it’s responding to support requests,
implementing custom code enhancements, or providing best practices for
data security, our team ensures you get the most out of your Microsoft
Power BI investment.

Our Microsoft Power BI support services

Troubleshooting & technical support

Resolve issues quickly with our dedicated support team. We handle
support requests related to:

-   Report performance issues and slow-loading dashboards

-   Power Query errors and data refresh failures

-   Security and access control settings

-   Power BI Service connectivity issues

Performance optimisation

Ensure optimal performance of your Microsoft Power BI solution with our
expert tuning services. We help businesses:

-   Optimise Power BI models for efficiency

-   Reduce load times for reports and dashboards

-   Implement best practices for data warehouse integration

-   Improve query performance using Power Query

Data security & compliance

Protect your organisation’s sensitive data with robust security
measures. Our Power BI support services cover:

-   Role-based access control and data governance

-   Compliance with industry regulations

-   Secure connections to on-premises and cloud-based data sources

-   Advanced security configurations for Power BI Service

Report development & customisation

Our Power BI experts assist customers with the following:

-   Creating and customising new dashboards and reports

-   Developing interactive reports for data-driven decision-making

-   Implementing custom code for advanced report functionality

-   Enhancing Power BI Service features to support business growth

Ongoing maintenance & support plans

regulatory requirements while ensuring secure document management across
multiple teams.

data access controls, ensuring only authorised users could view

Care Providers

A care provider needed a better way to track patient data, manage
resource allocation, and comply with healthcare regulations.

Solution: By optimising Power BI reports and implementing automated data
refresh schedules, we delivered real-time visibility into patient care
metrics. This enabled the organisation to improve staff allocation,
enhance the quality of care, and maintain compliance with healthcare
regulations.

Financial Services

A financial institution struggled with fragmented data across multiple

financial metrics, risk exposure, and regulatory compliance, while
automating data validation and streamlining reporting workflows to
support better decision-making and strengthen risk management.

Still have Qs?

Questions we often get asked:

How do I submit a Power BI support request?

You can submit a support ticket through our online portal or contact
members of our support team directly via phone or email. Our team will
assess your issue and provide prompt assistance.

Do you provide assistance for Power BI integrations?

Yes, we support Power BI integrations with various data sources,
including SQL Server, data warehouses, cloud services, and third-party
business intelligence tools.

Can you help optimise Power BI dashboards for better performance?

Absolutely. We help businesses enhance Power BI dashboard performance by
optimising data models, reducing load times, and implementing best
practices for report development.

What’s included in your Power BI support plans?

Our support plans include troubleshooting, security management,
performance monitoring, report development, and ongoing expert
assistance to ensure the smooth operation of your Power BI solution.

Do you offer training for Power BI users?

Yes, we provide tailored Power BI training sessions to help users
understand best practices, improve report-building skills, navigate and
fully leverage Power BI features.

Can you assist with Power BI security and compliance?

Yes, our team ensures your Power BI environment is secure and compliant
with industry regulations by implementing role-based access controls,
data encryption, and governance frameworks.
